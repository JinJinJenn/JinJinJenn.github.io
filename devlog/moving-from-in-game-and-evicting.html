Two more ways to move in

6 Nov 2026 · testing

MoveAnywhere had already passed its tests through the world map, but players can also move a household from inside the game or choose a lot where people already live. Both cases needed testing before calling it done.

Moving from inside the game

The plan was "use the phone". The phone has no such option in this version of the game, so the robot scrolled the whole Household menu to be sure. The in-game move turned out to be a Sim interaction that opens the same map as the world screen ("Moving with: Taylor"). The test triggers it directly, picks the park, and confirms.

The park was selectable and the household arrived and lived there without a "Building Lot" screen. Household funds and build mode worked normally, and all 15 checks passed.

Evicting people who live there

Moving onto an occupied lot makes the game ask whether to move in with the residents or evict them. In the initial eviction test, the household count stayed the same and all five evicted Sims remained available in household management, but they no longer had a home. The later update below adds automatic rehousing where a suitable empty house is available.

Update, a few hours later: the three loose ends are tied

  • A household with very little money couldn't move in because the game greys out the confirm button when the family can't afford the lot. The mod now shows the move screen a large balance while it's open, then restores the real amount afterward without charging the household for the lot.
  • When we tested a save where a household already lived in a park, the park still loaded as a park the first time. Converting it as soon as the save loads, rather than a little later, fixed that.
  • An evicted household now moves automatically into a vacant, furnished house, preferably one nearby with a couple of bedrooms. If the town has no empty house, the Sims remain homeless rather than being deleted.

Each of these has a robot test that passed on a copy of a save.
